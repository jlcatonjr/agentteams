# Part VI — The tamper-evident seal

<!-- skeleton:SB18 SB19 -->

The locks themselves carry a **tamper-evident seal** — like the little sticker on a medicine bottle. If
someone quietly weakens a lock (say, removes the part that blocks phoning out), the seal is broken and a
routine check flags it. When another team borrows the Linux doorkeeper, they keep an *identical* copy and
match its seal, so the two never quietly drift apart.

> One honest word: a seal makes tampering **obvious**, not **impossible**. It tells you *that* something
> changed; it doesn't physically prevent a determined person from changing it.
